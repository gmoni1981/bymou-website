TOSS CLUB

Brand Strategy · Naming · Visual Identity · Packaging · Art Direction · Campaign

TOSS CLUB is a ready-to-eat salad brand built around a simple idea: eating well should never feel boring.

We developed a bold, playful identity that transforms everyday salads into something people genuinely crave.

The challenge was to move beyond the predictable visual language of healthy food. Instead of relying on green palettes and conventional wellness imagery, we created a colorful, expressive brand inspired by youth culture, food cravings and a little bad behaviour.

Three signature salads—Crunch Club, Green Goddess and Spicy Romance—each bring their own personality to a unified packaging system.

Oversized typography, distinctive colors, expressive graphics and authentic lifestyle photography give TOSS CLUB an unmistakable identity across packaging, advertising and digital experiences.

We wanted salads to feel less like the responsible choice and more like the thing everyone wants.

GOOD FOOD. BAD MANNERS.

We developed a bold, expressive typography system that gives TOSS CLUB its unmistakable personality. Inspired by the energy of youth culture, playful food packaging and the irresistible nature of cravings, the lettering is built around oversized proportions, exaggerated curves and unconventional shapes.

Soft, rounded forms give the identity a sense of appetite and playfulness, while the heavy, confident letterforms bring attitude and visual impact. Rather than following the clean, restrained typography traditionally associated with healthy food, we wanted every word to feel loud, delicious and a little rebellious.

Typography became the voice of TOSS CLUB—turning a simple salad brand into a bold statement.
