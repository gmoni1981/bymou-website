Circulation and vertical connection

Entry → common lobby → covered court arcade → living, kitchen and guest suites. The rear dining area connects to the kitchen and pantry.

One continuous U-stair connects ground, upper and roof. The upper gallery joins the front and rear halls; each bedroom, office and family lounge has its own entrance.

Stair proposal: 3.24 m floor-to-floor, 18 risers of 180 mm, eight 260 mm treads per flight, 1.02 m turn landing and two 1.05 m-wide flights. Upper and roof slabs contain real openings.

Checked: area arithmetic and schematic access to all named rooms, using a 0.10 m grid and 0.20 m body radius against walls, door gaps, pool and principal furniture. Full walking-camera, door-swing and detailed headroom checks follow approval.

Identity retained

Central water and planting, tadelakt arches, warm stone, timber joinery, linen, aged bronze, soft daylight and a planted roof terrace. Courtyard-facing rooms and end halls use proposed closeable glazing to keep indoor and outdoor areas distinct.

05 / What requires your approval

  • Proposed depth increases from the reference chain's inferred 11.40 m to 16.70 m, retaining the 11.20 m width.
  • Four bedrooms replace the reference material's two-bedroom program: primary, Bedroom 2 and two guest suites.
  • 299.88 m² indoors plus 24 m² covered outdoors — these are explicitly separate allowances.
  • The 1.20 m upper west gallery shelters part of the court. The ground court is 4.20 m wide; the uninterrupted sky opening above is 3.00 m wide.
  • The main stair is proposed at the front-left, with continuation to the roof. This differs from the inconsistent reference stair positions.
  • Garden planting is inside the courtyard and roof. An external garden cannot be dimensioned without a plot boundary.
Detailed planning notes
  • Indoor courtyard boundaries have proposed closeable glazing, including glazed doors in the arches and end halls.
  • All new room arrangements, depth, floor heights and upper-floor dimensions are proposals, not surveyed facts.
  • Room-zone areas are approximate clear planning areas, not net usable room certificates; suite zones include bathrooms and internal partitions. Do not add this room table to calculate gross floor area.
  • Enclosed gross area includes enclosing/internal walls and stair allocation at each level. Covered outdoor galleries/arcades are reported separately.
  • The exterior garden boundary and total plot area are unknown; garden planting is contained in the courtyard for this study.
  • Upper west gallery shelters 12m² of the ground courtyard. Only 3.0m × 10.0m is open to the sky.
  • Pool retains the supplied 1.6m × 4.2m surface. Landscape beds are proposed at its ends, keeping 1.2m nominal continuous side routes.
  • Upper and roof slabs have explicit 3.10 × 2.20 stair openings. Stair clear vertical separation is 3.06m between matching flight tread surfaces and their 180mm thick treads above.
  • 18 risers × 180mm = 3.24m floor-to-floor. Two flights, 8 treads × 260mm per flight and 1.02m turn landing. Stair headroom must be checked in the detailed model before walking footage.
  • Four bedrooms are proposed: primary, Bedroom 2 and two ground-floor guest suites. This expands the two-bedroom references.
  • The open roof terrace gross plate is 147.24m² before parapets, planting and furniture; no roof terrace area is counted as enclosed house area.
