## Area check

- Dimension-chain envelope: **11.20 × 11.40 = 127.68 m²** per full rectangular level.
- Ground + one upper level, even without any courtyard: **255.36 m²** maximum rectangular floorplate sum.
- Subtracting the courtyard and voids makes this smaller. Thus this envelope cannot support 300 m² on two levels.
- The **4.20 m** dimension is a central plan band; clear courtyard width after wall treatment is not independently surveyed.
- **Pool surface: 4.20 × 1.60 = 6.72 m²**, supported by the handwritten annotation.
- Total plot area, setbacks, garden boundary, upper-floor extents, bathroom arrangement, room-specific clear dimensions and stair headroom are **not established** by the supplied material.

## Proposal rules

The approval study must label all new depth, upper-floor rooms, bathrooms, office, circulation, wall thicknesses and vertical heights as **proposed**. Retain the courtyard character, central pool and west public / east bedroom organization. Show gross built areas separately from net room areas and open landscape. Do not silently count open courtyard, pool, roof terrace or external garden as enclosed built area.

A 300 m² target alone cannot determine plot area. Any external garden remains outside the verified boundary until plot information exists. Internal courtyard planting can be dimensioned within the proposed building envelope.
