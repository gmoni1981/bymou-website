## Final photographic prompt set

### CITY / SKIN — hero
Create one exceptional editorial fashion photograph for an original speculative portfolio project CITY / SKIN. Landscape 3:2, photograph only, no lettering or logos. A fictional North African woman in her late sixties with short silver cropped hair, authentic expressive aged skin, strong self-possessed gaze and no sunglasses stands at an ordinary urban bus stop. Three-quarter body composition with her on the right half. Her sharply tailored wearable cobalt-blue suit is constructed from dense geometric public-transport-seat jacquard with oxidized green and white threads. Through the city bus window behind her, the SAME recognizable woven geometric upholstery is clearly visible on the seats: the city's overlooked fabric becomes fashion. Powerful independent fashion magazine art direction, not a costume or joke. Slightly low camera, direct flash, overcast daylight, tactile real glass reflections and street grit. Convincing tailoring, natural anatomy, real weight and seams. Left half contains graphic shapes of bus window and dark green vehicle surface useful for separately designed editorial typography. No generated text, collage, generic beauty model, luxury interior, architecture spectacle, puffer, beauty filtering or likeness to Mounia El. Make it feel like a deliberate fashion photograph with one memorable strategic idea.
Source: exec-114b693a-e1d2-40fb-bb15-d42b50b076c5.png.

### UNFLATTEN — hero
One extraordinary conceptual advertising PHOTOGRAPH, landscape 3:2, for a fictional sparkling citrus drink project UNFLATTEN. No lettering anywhere: typography will be designed separately. Low close camera across the scratched brushed-stainless-steel folding table of an ordinary contemporary laundromat. One unbranded saturated tangerine-orange slim aluminum drink can, open pull tab and real condensation, stands upright toward the left third on the flat part of the table. Immediately to its right the continuous metal tabletop rises into one huge, smooth, taut wave, 35 cm tall, then falls back to flat. The metal itself seems to inhale: one physically impossible but photographically convincing material deformation. Show the unbroken table front edge curving with the wave, scratch detail, realistic reflected fluorescent strip lights. One neatly folded white cotton T-shirt rests beyond the wave near the right edge anchoring scale. Cropped blurred circular washing machine doors in background, cool practical light and controlled direct editorial flash. Orange, steel and deep plum shadows. Distinctive modern advertising art direction, tactile and immediate. No floating fruit, no people, no luxury interior, no CGI shine, no generic studio product pedestal. The can must have realistic proportions and aluminum rim, the wave must read as the same single continuous tabletop, not a loose cloth or ribbon. Large confident composition with room for separate headline. No text/logos/watermarks/collage.
Source: exec-1e36b6e5-575b-4653-81ea-a4fe3e892ab1.png.

### CITY / SKIN — material
Reference: the accepted hero. Companion image to the supplied CITY / SKIN editorial photograph. Same physical cobalt, oxidized green and ivory geometric jacquard textile, same sharply tailored suit. Make a distinct close-up fashion photograph of the jacket's notch lapel, beautifully made buttonhole and precisely stitched shoulder seam on the older woman's body. Crop from below her chin to the top of her hip, no face in frame. One natural aged hand resting at the waist, silver simple ring. Textile fibers tactile and substantial, the pattern and lapel construction crisp. Behind is an unfocused dark green bus body. Flash editorial photograph with exact material continuity to reference. Landscape 3:2. No text, logos, graphic layout, beauty retouch or different garment. This is a material study for the same fashion story, not another full-body portrait.
Source: exec-86cde62a-88e5-4439-8b71-8d51ca0b8935.png.

### UNFLATTEN — sensory detail
Reference: the accepted hero. A single very close editorial product photograph for the same fictional sparkling citrus drink as reference. Distinct composition from reference: overhead macro, orange unbranded slim can's opened aluminum pull tab and cold condensation fill the lower right two thirds; on upper left, one cut tangerine segment rests on scratched stainless steel with visible wet pulp. Frame crops deeply through the can's shoulder. Extraordinary tactile detail, cold flash, punchy orange and reflective silver with deep plum shadows. Landscape 3:2. Authentic aluminum, satisfying wet citrus texture, physically plausible surface contact. No generated lettering, logos, typography, collage or extra props. This photograph will anchor a sensory slide in a deliberately designed social campaign. Match reference orange finish and material, but do not repeat its wide laundromat scene.
