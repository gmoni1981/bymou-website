## 01 — CITY / SKIN

**Central idea:** The city's overlooked materials can become its most distinctive fashion language. An independent AI casting and fashion editorial that turns a familiar urban textile into a character's unmistakable wardrobe.

**Intended impact:** An arresting person, a materially convincing garment and an unexpected connection to the place around her. Sophisticated, self-possessed and culturally specific; no celebrity imitation or generic beauty casting.

**Hero image:** A fictional woman in her late sixties, silver cropped hair and an unretouched, expressive face, photographed at an ordinary city bus stop. Her precisely tailored cobalt suit uses the same geometric jacquard as the bus seats visible behind her. The contrast between an overlooked everyday material and exceptional tailoring is the idea. No sunglasses, no likeness to Mounia, no luxury set, no oversized puffer, no comic costume.

**Graphic system:** A custom CITY / SKIN title with a single seam-like division; compact garment-label information; cobalt, oxidized green and warm white drawn from the scene. The seam controls image crops, text alignment and transitions. The character remains free of type across her face.

**Supporting work:** One close tactile image of the same garment's seam and woven textile. A short editorial sequence connects place → material → character. A mobile cover and a sequential reveal use different compositions and locally authored typography, not repeated templates. No invented biography or testimonial.

**Presentation:** A nearly full-screen portrait-led opening, an asymmetric type-and-material spread, then a controlled full-body reveal. The crop opens along the seam as the reader moves down the case. The static images remain photographs in the presentation; no suggestion of a fabricated fashion film.

**Reference principle:** Church's relationship between an expressive identity and restrained supporting information; Vevo's insistence that the visual subject remains central. No borrowed lettering, layout or garments.

**Generation plan:** One hero photograph, inspected first; then one genuinely different close material study using the accepted hero as a reference. Two images maximum for this initial case. All titles, labels, layouts and mobile adaptations authored locally.

**Proposed hero prompt, not executed:**
> Create one editorial fashion photograph, horizontal 4:3 with room for a vertical crop. A fictional North African woman in her late sixties, unmistakably silver cropped hair, bare face with authentic age and skin texture, strong self-possessed gaze, no sunglasses, stands at an ordinary contemporary city bus stop. She wears a precisely constructed cobalt-blue tailored suit in dense geometric public-transport-seat jacquard, with oxidized green and white threads. The same recognizable textile is visible on seats through the bus window behind her. Make the relationship between familiar upholstery and extraordinary tailoring intentional and immediately legible. The garment is wearable couture with convincing seams, weight and sleeve construction, never a novelty costume or puffer. Documentary direct flash, real glass reflections and street texture, adult independent-fashion editorial, carefully balanced negative space on the left, natural anatomy. No Mounia likeness, celebrity likeness, generated writing, logos, watermarks, luxury interiors, architectural spectacle, smooth beauty-filter skin or floating decorative objects.

## 02 — UNFLATTEN YOUR DAY

**Central idea:** Turn the sensation of carbonation into a physical change in an ordinary day. A speculative sparkling-citrus campaign whose entire world follows one rule: releasing pressure makes a flat surface rise.

**Intended impact:** A small product action causes one impossible but photographically convincing event. The image should communicate the proposition before a viewer reads the headline.

**Hero image:** A low, close composition at a working laundromat: a tangerine-orange can with a visibly open pull tab rests on a brushed-steel folding table. Immediately around its base, the steel rises into one taut, continuous wave, as if the surface has taken a breath. Fine scratches, reflected fluorescent light, table edges and contact shadows remain sharply believable. The can stands on the flat starting point; one folded white T-shirt rests on the far side of the wave and makes the changed scale immediately understandable. One impossible material behavior, no floating fruit or miscellaneous surreal props.

**Graphic system:** A compressed headline opens into a wider setting as pressure is released. One rising contour links the headline, crop and label. Tangerine, reflective silver and deep aubergine shadows belong to this campaign, not the BYMOU navigation. Design the can's simple campaign label separately; do not ask the model for a finished branded advertisement.

**Supporting work:** One macro of the opened can, condensation and actual citrus texture to anchor the sensory promise. A vertical poster introduces the compressed headline, reveals the physical image, then resolves the proposition. Three different narrative compositions, not three copies of an ad. No nutritional, recycling or efficacy claims.

**Presentation:** A wide cinematic key visual followed by a narrow tactile detail and a typographic pressure-release sequence. Any interactive deformation will use an authored mesh or SVG with a clear input-to-response relationship. A moving still is never labeled a generated video; new AI video is outside this plan.

**Reference principle:** Bose's sensory proposition translated into expression; Instrument's principle of selecting the presentation medium to fit the story. The physical event and its graphic contour are original proposals.

**Generation plan:** One hero photograph; inspect whether contact, material continuity and perspective work before continuing. One companion macro, referenced to the accepted can. Two images maximum initially. Typography, label, pressure behavior and social sequence authored locally.

**Proposed hero prompt, not executed:**
> One hyperreal conceptual advertising photograph, horizontal 16:9, low close camera at a working contemporary laundromat folding table. A single tangerine-orange slim aluminum sparkling-drink can, no writing or logo, visibly open pull tab, stands upright on brushed stainless steel. Immediately beyond its base the continuous tabletop rises into one large taut shallow wave, as if pressure released by the can has made a flat day expand. Preserve the table's real metal micro-scratches, unbroken side edge, plausible fluorescent light reflections and contact shadows across the curve. The can rests on the flat beginning of the surface. One neatly folded white cotton T-shirt rests beyond the raised section and anchors its scale. Cropped circular washing-machine doors remain softly out of focus in the deep background; this is a tactile advertising composition, not a photograph of an interior. Hard editorial flash meets cool practical light, warm tangerine against reflective silver and deep aubergine shadows. The one impossible behavior is the lifted metal surface; everything else has real weight, material and geometry. The product is immediately identifiable, the transformation legible, and the left third allows typography to be designed separately. No readable text, logos, stock packshot layout, floating fruit, multiple objects, plastic-looking CGI, decorative architecture, luxury setting, collage or people.


## 03 — OPEN END

**Central idea:** Culture is never finished. A speculative participatory arts publication whose identity becomes complete only when someone makes an edit.

**Intended impact:** An unmistakable graphic image that the visitor discovers they can author. This case proves design and creative technology through an actual usable system, with no photographic filler.

**Hero visual:** Huge custom OPEN / END stencil letterforms in black and warm white with a single vermilion opening. A visitor draws one path through the composition. That trace opens a controlled negative-space channel through the letters, revealing the colored layer beneath while preserving readability. An intentional, physical cut in the graphic identity.

**Graphic system:** Authored SVG glyphs, consistent stroke and counter rules, technical edit annotations and one accent. Every output carries the same typographic DNA; only the reader's cut changes. The identity extends to an editorial opener and a poster because those formats demonstrate the same act of editing.

**Supporting work:** The actual browser tool, two contrasting outputs exported from it, and one editorial spread explaining path → mask → final composition. A clear reset, three keyboard-accessible example paths and real SVG/PNG export. No fictitious user submissions or fake collective activity.

**Presentation:** Begin inside a full-width live poster; let the visitor edit it; reveal the underlying geometry and a small set of meaningful outputs. This case has the rhythm of a tool demonstration, not another image gallery. Provide a clear static and reduced-motion view.

**Reference principle:** Symphony's meaningful responsive identity, EA's usable creative tools, and the orientation preserved in Awwwards' Unseen gallery transitions. The letterforms and cut interaction are designed for BYMOU rather than reproducing those references.

**Generation plan:** Zero generated photographs. Create the graphic work in SVG/Canvas/HTML/CSS and capture real outputs. No generated image used to impersonate an interactive tool.
