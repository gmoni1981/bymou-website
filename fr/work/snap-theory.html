SNAP THEORY

Brand Strategy · Visual Identity · Packaging · Art Direction · Campaign

SNAP THEORY is a new-generation chocolate-covered protein snack brand built around one simple idea: good taste should never be a compromise.

BYMOU Studio developed the brand from scratch, creating its positioning, naming, visual identity, packaging system, illustration language, art direction, and campaign concept.

Bold typography, playful illustrations, expressive colors, and tactile food photography come together to create a distinctive identity designed for a new generation of snack lovers.

THE CREATIVE VISION

More than a brand. A world of its own.

At BYMOU Studio, we believe the most compelling brands don't simply communicate—they create worlds people want to be part of.

For SNAP THEORY, every detail was an opportunity to challenge the expected. From expressive typography and hand-drawn characters to tactile packaging, imaginative art direction and playful motion, we built a visual universe with its own personality.

Our approach brings together strategy, design, illustration, photography and emerging creative technologies. Each discipline informs the next, creating work that feels cohesive, distinctive and alive.

The result is more than a recognizable identity. It's a brand with character, consistency and the freedom to evolve across every touchpoint.

Creativity is not an added detail. It's the foundation of everything we make.
