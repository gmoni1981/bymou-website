Élan Pâtisserie

Brand Strategy / Identity / Hospitality

Élan Pâtisserie is a contemporary bakery and café concept imagined and developed from the ground up by BYMOU Studio. From brand strategy and naming to visual identity, packaging, art direction, and the physical environment, we created a complete brand experience that celebrates the simple pleasure of everyday indulgence.

Inspired by European café culture, Élan combines refined typography, warm materials, and thoughtful details to make every moment feel a little more special.

We developed a complete visual identity built around understated elegance and everyday warmth. A distinctive wordmark, expressive graphic symbols, tactile packaging, and a carefully considered palette of cream, terracotta, and cocoa brown create a recognizable brand across every touchpoint.

From pastry boxes and coffee cups to menus, signage, interiors, and digital campaigns, every detail was designed to feel effortlessly connected.

More than a pâtisserie, Élan is a complete hospitality experience—where identity, atmosphere, and the everyday ritual of good food come together.
