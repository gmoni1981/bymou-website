SCOOP CULT

Ice cream has always been about pleasure. But somewhere along the way, the better-for-you category started looking more like a supplement aisle than something you'd actually crave.

For SCOOP CULT, we imagined a different kind of protein ice cream brand. One that doesn't ask people to choose between indulgence and their lifestyle. A brand built around unapologetic cravings, individuality, and the belief that good taste should never be boring.

We developed a distinctive hand-drawn illustration system that brings SCOOP CULT’s playful personality to life. Inspired by the ingredients and cravings behind each flavor, our illustrations combine expressive lines, bold silhouettes, and vibrant two-tone colors to create a playful, unmistakable visual identity. From melting scoops and cookie sandwiches to berries, chocolate, and graphic symbols, the system brings personality and consistency to packaging, advertising, animation, and digital experiences.

We are BYMOU Studio, an independent creative studio specializing in brand strategy, art direction, visual identity, design, and AI-driven production—creating bold, distinctive experiences across digital and physical worlds.
