GOOD BUG is a premium sparkling prebiotic soda created to make gut-friendly living feel fresh, fun and effortlessly part of everyday life.

Made with fruit-inspired flavours and prebiotic fibre that helps nourish beneficial gut bacteria, GOOD BUG brings a playful new personality to functional beverages.

Our vision was to challenge the conventional language of wellness branding. We wanted to create a product that feels as desirable as it is purposeful—something people would proudly pick up, carry and share.

At BYMOU, we developed a vibrant visual identity built around expressive typography, mischievous fruit characters and three distinctive flavours: Strawberry Guava, Peach Passion and Cherry Lime.

From packaging and character design to lifestyle photography, advertising and motion, every element was created to bring GOOD BUG into contemporary Gen-Z culture.

The result is a feel-good beverage brand with personality, purpose and unmistakable shelf presence.
