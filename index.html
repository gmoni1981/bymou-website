MOU GRASSO / BYMOU
CREATIVE DIRECTION & ART DIRECTION

I'd rather make something unmistakable than simply beautiful.

I'm Mou Grasso, the creative director behind BYMOU.

Every project starts with a conversation. I want to understand what you're building, who you're speaking to, and what makes your business different.

From there, I shape the entire visual experience — brand identity, advertising films, creative campaigns, websites and architectural imagery.

I'm involved in the details, from the first idea to the final delivery. The light, the typography, the movement, the smallest decisions that make everything feel intentional.

I don't believe every brand should look like the latest trend. I believe the right creative direction should make people recognize yours.

Have something ambitious in mind? Let's talk↗
