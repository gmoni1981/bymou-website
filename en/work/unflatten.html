BYMOU / 2026 / Speculative concept study

unflatten.

Brand concept · Advertising · Social system

What if a small change could give your whole day another dimension?

The premise

Make the feeling
physically visible.

For a fictional sparkling-citrus brand, carbonation becomes an unexpected change in an ordinary day. One impossible material behavior makes the proposition immediate: open the can, and the everyday gains a dimension.

Two AI-created photographic elements meet authored typography and a single rising contour. The campaign avoids health claims, invented sales and fake launch results. The product and brand are speculative.

unflatten.

The visual system

Compressed everyday language opens into fluid italic lettering. Tangerine gives the idea energy; steel makes it tactile; deep plum gives the typography weight. The contour carries the same shift across every format.

A sequence.
Not five copies.

A campaign designed as a sequence: interrupt, reveal, make it felt, invite, resolve.
