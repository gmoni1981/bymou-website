## 01 — KERN

**Concept and capability.** Fictional modular desk-tools identity demonstrating brand strategy, identity design, packaging, product art direction, and a cohesive digital touchpoint system. KERN’s idea is that useful tools connect and adapt as work changes.

**Visual system.** Build an interlocking “K” mark from simple modular geometry. Use graphite, chalk, and signal green as the project palette. Set a precise, open sans-serif typographic hierarchy: strong condensed display labels, readable product descriptions, and restrained technical annotations. Apply consistent alignment, generous negative space, and a small set of modular rules across every surface.

**Deliverables and layout.** Identity suite (primary mark, compact mark, clear-space and misuse rules); three-piece product family (aluminum desk clip, modular tray, cable guide); recycled-card packaging system with front, back, and side panels; one product hero image; one material/detail image; one packaging flat-lay; a small responsive ecommerce landing-page concept; social launch tile; and a concise brand-guideline spread. Photograph objects on a chalk or graphite surface with soft directional light, crisp edge detail, and legible packaging. No unsupported sustainability certification or material claims.

No logos, letters, labels, watermark, extra products, or people. Product photography, landscape 4:3.”

**Case-study sequence and acceptance.** Open with the hero and identity rationale; follow with mark construction, typography/color, packaging system, product and detail views, ecommerce mobile/desktop, then guidelines. Avoid cartoon characters or child-coded styling.
