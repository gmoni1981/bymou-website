SPECULATIVE CAMPAIGN / FIVE FRAMES

deep cut.

Fictional brand · Speculative campaign by BYMOU. AI photographic elements; original typography, identity and layouts.

Less skipping. More getting lost in it.

The idea starts with a familiar habit: hearing the hook and moving on. DEEP CUT invites the opposite. Give a record a whole side, and let curiosity do the rest.

A sequence with a reason to swipe.

One voice. Five different moves.

A sleeve becomes a stage for the hook. A groove becomes a label. A circular crop follows a hand into the record bin. Then photography gives way to a listening ritual and a full brand reveal. Every frame moves the idea forward.

Instrument Serif brings the expressiveness of a record-store sleeve. A cut groove and a needle form the mark. Vermilion, lilac, ink and warm white keep the sequence connected without repeating a layout.

PHOTOGRAPHY / AI

Made as an independent BYMOU concept for a fictional brand. Three photographic elements were created with the built-in ChatGPT image tool. The copy, glyph outlines, mark, crops and five compositions were authored separately. No client commission, published campaign or performance results are claimed.
