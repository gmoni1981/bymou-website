BYMOU / 2026 / Speculative concept study

CITY / SKIN

Fashion editorial · AI casting · Art direction

The city has a dress code. We changed it.

The premise

Style isn’t a place.
It’s a point of view.

Public transport upholstery is designed to disappear into everyday life. This fashion study brings it into full view: the familiar weave becomes precise tailoring, worn by a character with presence rather than predictable youth.

The image and its material detail were created with AI. Casting, palette, editorial typography and the seam-based layout form one directed concept. This is an independent study, not an existing fashion commission.

The visual system

One seam.
Two worlds.

The slash becomes a seam: it divides the name, opens an image and separates utilitarian information from expressive editorial type. Cobalt, deep transit green and woven ivory come directly from the story.
